# ReForm — Waste, Reborn

**AI for Climate · Circular Economy & Waste Reduction · COP31**

ReForm is a research and pilot proposal exploring how selected, traceable PLA 3D-printing waste could be regenerated into reusable filament and practical products.

## Evidence and status disclosure
ReForm is a **research and pilot proposal**, not a validated recycling system. No completed ReForm material-processing experiments, qualified filament, validated AI predictions, confirmed laboratory partner, or measured net carbon savings are claimed.

- The prototype uses illustrative sample data and deterministic front-end logic.
- No backend, AI model or API is implemented.
- AI-assisted quality prediction and experiment prioritization are proposed future research tasks.
- Material release must depend on appropriate laboratory tests and human review.
- Climate benefits must be evaluated against an equivalent functional product and include collection, sorting, processing, energy, transport, yield losses and end-of-life.

## Proposed first pilot
1. Secure access to a qualified materials laboratory and suitable equipment.
2. Verify a clean, segregated, traceable PLA feedstock stream.
3. Define safety controls, test methods and acceptance criteria with qualified personnel.
4. Run controlled reprocessing trials against virgin commercial filament.
5. Measure yield, filament consistency, print reliability, application performance, energy and cost.
6. Develop AI decision support only after sufficient independent batch data exist.
7. Conduct a comparative life-cycle assessment before making net climate claims.
