# ReForm — Presenter Notes

**Disclosure:** The browser demo is a front-end concept using illustrative sample data and deterministic navigation. No material-processing experiment, AI model/API, lab validation or measured climate benefit is claimed.

1. Opening: ReForm explores how selected, traceable PLA waste can become useful 3D-printing filament.
2. Problem and users: campus makerspaces generate failed prints and supports. The challenge is verifying polymer identity, history and regenerated material quality.
3. Solution: explain the six-stage loop and batch passport.
4. Materials strategy: start with known-source PLA; PET and composites are separate future routes.
5. AI: proposed quality prediction and trial ranking. No AI model/API is selected or implemented.
6. Demo: show intake, preparation, quality hold and batch passport. RF-001 is illustrative and remains NOT RELEASED.
7. Pilot: one batch, one spool, one useful non-critical part. Compare with commercial control and measure yield, reliability, energy, transport and cost.
8. Close: seek a qualified materials lab, campus pilot and technical mentors.
