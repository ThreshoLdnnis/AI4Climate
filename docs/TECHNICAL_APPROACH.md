# Technical Approach & Disclosure

## Current prototype
- HTML, CSS and JavaScript front-end.
- Local browser interaction only; no backend or database.
- Four screens: intake, preparation, quality review and batch passport.
- Illustrative synthetic sample record RF-001.
- No external API or AI model is implemented.

## Proposed material route
Initial scope: segregated, known-source PLA printing waste. The research proposal is controlled thermomechanical reprocessing, followed by filament geometry and application tests. PET and waste-filled composites are separate future research routes requiring their own validation.

## Proposed AI research
Potential future tasks, subject to sufficient independent experimental data:
- Predict selected filament quality indicators from verified batch and process records.
- Flag uncertain or out-of-distribution batches for additional testing.
- Rank useful next experiments.
- Compare recommendations against fixed recipes and simple rule-based baselines.

AI recommendations remain decision support. Laboratory tests and qualified human review determine material release.

## Planned data categories
- Feedstock source, polymer identity, accepted and rejected mass.
- Preparation and formulation records.
- Operator-recorded processing conditions.
- Filament measurements and print/application test results.
- Energy, water, transport and consumables for environmental accounting.

No project-specific experimental dataset is claimed. Data collection depends on lab access, safe procedures, consent and agreed protocols.

## Evidence status
- Concept and proposed workflow: defined.
- Front-end concept prototype: prepared.
- Material processing and lab validation: not reported.
- AI model/API: not selected or implemented.
- Net climate benefit: not measured.
