## Evidence boundaries
- Published research does not establish ReForm's own performance.
- Recycled mass alone is not proof of avoided emissions.
- Compare equivalent functional products and include collection, sorting, processing, energy, transport, yield losses, product lifetime and end-of-life.
- Future AI capabilities must not be represented as implemented.
