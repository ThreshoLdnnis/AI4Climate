# Project Overview — ReForm

## One-line concept
ReForm explores a controlled circular-manufacturing workflow that converts selected, traceable PLA 3D-printing waste into reusable filament and practical products.

## Problem
Campus makerspaces and fabrication labs may generate failed prints, support structures and clean PLA offcuts. Reuse is constrained by uncertainty about polymer identity, contamination, processing history and regenerated filament quality.

## Proposed users
- Campus makerspaces and fabrication labs
- Student design and prototyping teams
- Potential local material recovery partners after a validated pilot

## Proposed workflow
1. Collect and verify known-source PLA.
2. Sort, clean and prepare feedstock.
3. Conduct controlled thermomechanical reprocessing trials.
4. Extrude filament and measure quality.
5. Print a defined non-critical product and test fit for purpose.
6. Record use and requalify material before another cycle.

## Potential climate contribution
If regenerated filament reliably substitutes for virgin filament in an equivalent application, ReForm may reduce virgin material demand and divert suitable PLA waste from disposal. This is a hypothesis until material performance and comparative life-cycle impacts are measured.
